# Amazon importer and 1099-K tie-out

## Amazon settlement import
Imports one Amazon **settlement report** (flat file, tab-separated; Seller Central → Payments → Reports repository). Built from Amazon's documented column names and the made-up file in `samples/amazon/`; real reports still need testing (backlog). Code: `src/shared/amazon.ts` (reading, classifying, planning; pure), `src/shared/amazonImport.ts` (types), `src/main/amazonImport.ts`, screen `AmazonImport.tsx` (company home → **Import from Amazon**). Same approach as Etsy (`etsy-import.md`); channel `amazon`.

- **Reading**: columns found by name (settlement-id, settlement-start-date, settlement-end-date, deposit-date, total-amount, currency, transaction-type, order-id, amount-type, amount-description, amount, posted-date or posted-date-time, sku). The first data row without an amount type is the settlement summary (dates, payout total). Dates: 2026-03-14, "2026-03-14 07:32:11 UTC", ISO with time, 03/14/2026, 14.03.2026. Decimal commas are accepted. Non-USD and unreadable rows are listed and left out; zero rows skipped.
- **Kinds** (`classifyAmazonRow`): ItemPrice Principal → sales (refund rows → refunds), Shipping/GiftWrap → shipping (refund rows → refunds), any tax and ItemWithheldTax / MarketplaceFacilitator → sales tax (nets to zero), Promotion → promotions, ItemFees Commission/RefundCommission → referral fees, FBA/fulfilment → FBA fees, shipping/label → shipping labels, other ItemFees → other fees, Subscription → subscription, storage/removal/disposal → FBA fees, advertising → advertising, reimbursements/reversals → other money in, reserve rows → **reserve** (not posted: the money stays in the Amazon payment account), anything else → unknown ("Other Amazon activity", listed with examples).
- **Posting** (`planAmazon`): one entry per posted date (lines combined per account; each row's amount credited to its account, the day's total debited to the Amazon payment account; memo "Amazon activity DATE: N orders, N refunds (settlement ID)"), plus the **payout** as a transfer from the Amazon payment account to the bank on the deposit date (only when the total is positive; a negative settlement shows a note that Amazon will charge the card). The payout is recorded once per settlement (`payout|<id>`), so re-importing never doubles it.
- **Accounts** (`AMAZON_ACCOUNTS`, added by **Add these accounts**): 1220 Amazon payment account (note), 2220 Sales tax collected by Amazon (note), 6111 Amazon referral fees, 6112 Amazon FBA fees (note), 6113 Amazon other fees (subscription, other, unknown; note), 6003 Amazon advertising. Defaults by number: Sales 4000, Shipping income 4010, promotions and refunds 4090, shipping labels 6950, other money in 4900. Choices are saved per kind (`channel_mappings`, channel `amazon`).
- The screen shows the settlement id and period, rows new / already imported / before the books start, the payout, the reserve held back or released, unreadable and unrecognized rows, totals per kind with account boxes, the payout account, an accountant note, and **Amazon payouts and your bank** (found / waiting in review / not in the bank yet; same table as Etsy).

## 1099-K tie-out (`src/main/form1099k.ts`, `src/shared/form1099k.ts`, `Form1099K.tsx`)
- Company home → **1099-K tie-out**. For a year: one row each for Etsy and Amazon (imported gross = sales, shipping and sales tax buyers paid on the imported rows, before fees and refunds: `marketplace_rows.gross_cents`; "By month" shows the 12 months), plus any other platform the owner adds (PayPal, Square…; amount only).
- The owner types box 1a from each 1099-K and notes; the difference (form − imported) is shown, and when any difference exists, the usual reasons with a "Check with your accountant" note. Clearing the amount removes the row (`form_1099k`, one per year and platform, audited).

## Database (schema v12)
- `marketplace_rows.gross_cents` (gross receipts per imported row; Etsy sale and sales-tax rows, Amazon order item-price rows).
- `form_1099k`: year, platform (unique pair), gross_cents, notes. Audited.
